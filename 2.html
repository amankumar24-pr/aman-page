Scambled Eggs

Eggs are one of my favourite foods. Here is arecipe for deliciuusly rich scrambled eggs.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1tbs butter
  • 2tbs cream

Mettod

  1. Melt butter in a friying pan over a medium heat
  2. Gently mix the eggs and cream in a bowl
  3. Once butter has melted add cream and eggs
  4. Using a spatula fold the eggs from the edge of the pan the center every 20 seconds (as if you are making an complette)
  5. Whwn the eggs are still moist remove from the heat( It Will continue to cook on the plate untill served)
